We currently use two types of cameras with various lens kits:

- Sony Burano (main camera)
- Sony FX6 (second unit, for close-ups: no genlock input, so not used on the LED wall)
- RED Komodo 6K (third unit)
- RED Gemini 5K
- Zeiss CP3 Lens Kit
